# PortReaper Icon Finalists

Six finalist variations across three directions, built on Apple's 1024×1024 icon grid (824 px plate, 100 px margin, 185 px corner radius) using PortReaper's tokens (`#FF4528` Signal Coral on `#121316` Tactical Obsidian, `#0A0B0E` Void Black for Dark, and a single-colour foreground glyph layer `glyph-*.svg` for macOS 26 Icon Composer Clear/Tinted).

## 1. Ghost in the Socket

- **`icon-1a.svg` (`glyph-1a.svg`, `tray-1a.svg`)**: The uncut Ethernet socket houses a tall, faceless ghost hollow with a semicircular arch head, vertical sides, and three soft sinusoidal waves at its hem. The bold stepped-socket outer silhouette and 348 px-wide central hollow create a high-contrast dark core at 16 px while the three rounded waves read clearly at 32 px.
- **`icon-1b.svg` (`glyph-1b.svg`, `tray-1b.svg`)**: The faceless ghost hollow flares outward into a 404 px billowing skirt with deeper sinusoidal waves so the zombie fills nearly 60% of the port. Widening the hollow to 404 px (6.3 px at 16×16) and deepening the wave troughs to 70 px preserves the flared ghost cavity and bottom undulation even at 16 px.

## 2. Reaper Crescent

- **`icon-2a.svg` (`glyph-2a.svg`, `tray-2a.svg`)**: A sweeping crescent scythe blade curls around the left of a compact, solid Ethernet port socket. Two solid, high-mass shapes (a 156 px-thick crescent and a solid stepped socket) separated by a 136 px dark channel stay completely distinct at 16 px.
- **`icon-2b.svg` (`glyph-2b.svg`, `tray-2b.svg`)**: A diagonal scythe crescent arches overhead around the top-left of a ringed Ethernet port socket tucked in the lower-right pocket. The diagonal 10:30 crescent hugs the squircle corner while leaving a 2 px dark gap and a 224×100 px inner socket cutout that still registers at 16 px.

## 3. Cut Socket (Refined)

- **`icon-3a.svg` (`glyph-3a.svg`, `tray-3a.svg`)**: Today's cut socket refined with heavier 106 px walls, a 50% wider 132 px curved scythe gap, and a broadened 92 px left latch foot with no thin sliver. At 16 px, the 132 px cut spans over 2 full pixels of dark space from top to bottom so the two C-brackets never bleed together.
- **`icon-3b.svg` (`glyph-3b.svg`, `tray-3b.svg`)**: The scythe arc tapers like a real blade (164 px entry to 128 px exit) and shears the two severed socket halves by 18 px along the cut. The wider tapered channel (> 2.2 px at 16×16) combined with the vertical step offset across the cut makes the 'sliced in two' action unmistakable at 16 px.
